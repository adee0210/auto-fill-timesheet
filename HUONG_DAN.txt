AUTO FILL TIMESHEET - HUONG DAN SU DUNG
=======================================

1. Giai nen file AutoFillTimesheet.zip ra mot thu muc bat ky.
2. Bam dup AutoFillTimesheet.exe de mo app
   (co the chuot phai > Send to > Desktop (create shortcut) de tao shortcut).
3. Nhap ten dang nhap, mat khau, khoang ngay, gio lam, ngay nghi le
   (moi dong 1 ngay, dinh dang DD.MM.YYYY).
4. Bam "Tai danh sach project/activity tu he thong" (lan dau),
   sau do chon Project va Activity can dien.
5. Bam "Luu & chay". App se mo trinh duyet Chrome/Edge va tu dong dien.

6. Xoa entry: nhap ngay vao o "Ngay can xoa" (moi dong 1 ngay DD.MM.YYYY
   hoac khoang DD.MM.YYYY-DD.MM.YYYY), bam "Xoa toan bo entry cua cac ngay nay",
   xac nhan. App se tich "Erase Entry" va bam OK cho tung entry.

Yeu cau: may da cai Google Chrome hoac Microsoft Edge, co ket noi mang.
Lan chay dau tien co the cham hon vi Selenium tu tai driver cho trinh duyet.

Bao mat: thong tin dang nhap luu trong account.json canh file .exe.
Khong gui thu muc nay cho nguoi khac sau khi da nhap mat khau.
Neu Windows SmartScreen canh bao, bam "More info" > "Run anyway".
